These terms apply when you use RaktDoot to find blood, sign up as a donor, or visit this website. By using RaktDoot you agree to them. Please read them with our privacy policy.

What RaktDoot is

RaktDoot is a helpline. An AI voice agent calls blood banks near a hospital to find the blood a patient needs and asks them to hold it. If no bank has it, the agent calls donors nearby who agreed to be called.

RaktDoot does not store, test, supply or sell blood, and it does not give medical advice. Blood banks decide whether to hold or issue blood, under their own rules and the law.

Not an emergency service

In a medical emergency, call 112, or 108 for an ambulance, before anything else. We cannot promise that blood will be found, held or still available when you arrive, or that a donor will come.

Asking for blood

  • Ask only for a real patient who needs blood, and give correct details: the group, the component, the units and the hospital.
  • A hold is the bank's promise, not ours. Go to the bank before the hold ends, with the papers it asks for, usually the doctor's request form and the patient's blood sample.
  • Tell us if you no longer need the blood, so banks and donors are not kept waiting.

Signing up as a donor

  • You must be at least 18 and sign up with your own mobile number.
  • By signing up, you agree that RaktDoot's AI agent may call that number about blood requests near you. You can opt out at any time, as the privacy policy explains.
  • Saying yes on a call is not a promise. You can change your mind, and the blood bank decides whether you can donate after its own checks.
  • Donation is voluntary and unpaid. No one from RaktDoot will ever ask you for money.

AI calls

Our calls are made by an AI, and each call says so at the start. The AI can mishear or make mistakes. Check important details, such as the bank's address and the time a hold ends, before you travel.

Fees

Families and donors pay RaktDoot nothing. Hospitals and blood banks that use RaktDoot pay under separate agreements. Blood banks may charge a processing fee for each unit, which the government caps; RaktDoot does not take any part of it.

What you must not do

  • Make false requests, or use RaktDoot to reach, harass or contact donors outside it.
  • Sign up someone else's number as a donor.
  • Try to get at donor details, disrupt the service, or use it to sell blood.

We may block requests or numbers that break these terms, and report misuse to the authorities.
