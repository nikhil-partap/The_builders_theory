Privacy policy

Last updated 22 November 2026

RaktDoot helps families find blood by calling blood banks and, when no bank has stock, donors who signed up. This page explains what we collect, why, who sees it, and how you can opt out or have it deleted.

What we collect

When you ask for blood

  • The blood group, the component and the number of units needed.
  • The hospital's name, which we use to find nearby blood banks.
  • A phone number, if you give one, so we can tell you where the blood is held.

We do not ask for the patient's name or medical records.

When you sign up as a donor

  • Your name, blood group and area.
  • Your mobile number.
  • The time you agreed to AI calls, and the times we last called you.

When we place a call

  • Who was called, when, and the outcome, for example "no stock" or "holding 2 units until 11:40".
  • A written transcript of the call. Our voice-call provider may also keep an audio recording while it processes the call.

When you visit this website

The site uses no advertising or tracking cookies. Our hosting provider keeps standard server logs, such as IP addresses and the pages requested, to keep the service running and secure.

How AI calls work

RaktDoot's calls are made by an AI voice agent, not a person. Every call starts by saying it comes from RaktDoot's AI assistant. The agent asks blood banks about stock and asks them to hold units; it asks donors whether they can donate. It never asks anyone for money or for bank or card details.

We call a donor only after they have signed up and agreed to AI calls, only for their own blood group, only when the request is within about 10 km of them, at most once a week, and not for three months after they give blood.

How we use it

  • To find, check and hold blood for a request.
  • To call donors who agreed to be called, when no bank has stock.
  • To show a family the progress of their request.
  • To prevent misuse, fix problems and improve the service.

We do not sell personal data, and we do not use it for advertising.

Who sees it

  • Families see the banks we called and the outcome. They never see a donor's name or number; a confirmed donor appears as "Donor 1", with a distance.
  • Blood banks hear the details of a request on the call: the group, the component, the units and the hospital.
  • Donors hear that a patient nearby needs their blood group, and where to go.
  • Our service providers process data for us under contract: Serverpod Cloud hosts the service and its database, and our voice-call provider places the calls and turns speech into text.
  • Authorities, if the law requires it.

How long we keep it

  • Requests, call outcomes and transcripts: 90 days, then deleted or made anonymous.
  • Donor details: until you opt out or ask us to delete them.
  • Server logs: as long as our hosting provider keeps them, usually a few weeks.

Your choices

Stop the calls

You can opt out of donor calls at any time. Write to us from any address with the mobile number you signed up with, and we stop calling it.

See, correct or delete your data

You can ask for a copy of what we hold about you, ask us to correct it, or ask us to delete it. Write to us with your mobile number. We may call or message that number once to confirm the request is yours, and we act on it within 30 days.

Withdraw consent

Withdrawing your consent to AI calls is the same as opting out. It does not affect calls made before you withdrew it.

We handle personal data under India's Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023. If you are not satisfied with our answer, you may complain to the Data Protection Board of India.

Security

Data travels over encrypted connections and is stored with our hosting provider. Donor numbers are never sent to the RaktDoot app or shown on screen; only our servers use them to place calls. Only the people who run RaktDoot can reach the database.

Age

Donors must be at least 18 years old, the minimum age to give blood in India. We do not knowingly sign up anyone younger.

Changes to this policy

If we change how we use your data, we will update this page and its date. For a significant change, we will tell signed-up donors before it takes effect.

Contact us

For anything about your data or this policy, write to us.
